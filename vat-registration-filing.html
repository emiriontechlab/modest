UNDERSTANDING VAT IN UAE

What do you need to know about VAT in the UAE?

01

What is UAE VAT?

VAT is a 5% tax on most goods and services. Businesses charge VAT on their sales (output VAT) and recover VAT paid on business purchases (input VAT). You pay the FTA the difference each tax period.

02

Who must register for VAT?

You must register when your taxable supplies and imports pass AED 375,000 in the last 12 months, or are expected to pass it in the next 30 days. You can choose to register from AED 187,500.

03

What is zero-rated VAT?

Zero-rated supplies are taxed at 0%, and you can still recover input VAT on related costs. Examples: exports, international transport, and certain healthcare and education services.

04

What is exempt VAT?

Exempt supplies carry no VAT, and you cannot recover input VAT on related costs. Examples: certain financial services, residential rent after the first supply, bare land and local passenger transport.

05

What are the FTA penalties?

The FTA charges fixed fines for late registration and late returns, and percentage penalties for errors and late payment. The amounts are set by Cabinet decision and change over time, so check the current list on the FTA website. The easiest way to avoid them is to register and file on time.

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED

What documents do you need for VAT registration in the UAE?

Standard documents:

  • Trade licence copy
  • Passport and Emirates ID of the owner or authorised signatory
  • Contact details: email, phone and business address
  • Bank details: IBAN and bank name
  • Turnover for the last 12 months, or a 30-day forecast with supporting documents

Extra documents if they apply:

  • Memorandum of association, for companies with more than one shareholder
  • Customs registration number, for importers and exporters
  • Contracts or invoices, if you are registering based on expected supplies

Most businesses file every quarter. The FTA can set a monthly period for some businesses. Each return and any VAT due must reach the FTA by the 28th day of the month after the tax period ends.

The FTA charges a fixed fine for a late return, a higher fine for repeat late returns, and separate penalties on VAT paid late. The amounts are set by Cabinet decision and are listed on the FTA website. We track your deadlines so your return goes in on time.
