Types of UAE trade license

What types of trade license does Modest Consulting Services handle?

01

Commercial trade license

Covers buying, selling, and distribution of goods. Suitable for trading companies, import-export businesses, retail operations, and e-commerce businesses.

02

Professional trade license

Covers service-based and professional activities. Suitable for consultancies, law firms, accounting practices, medical clinics, engineering firms, and IT companies.

03

Industrial trade license

Covers manufacturing and industrial activities. Suitable for factories, production facilities, and processing operations.

04

Tourism trade license

Covers travel agencies, tour operators, hotel management, and hospitality businesses.

05

Free zone trade license

Issued by the relevant free zone authority — JAFZA, DMCC, IFZA, SHAMS, RAKEZ, or other zones. Permits business operations within the free zone and internationally.

What documents do you need for a UAE trade license?

Standard documents:

  • Passport copy — all owners and managers
  • Emirates ID — UAE residents
  • UAE visa copy or entry stamp — if in UAE
  • Proposed trade name — 3 options
  • Business activity description
  • NOC from sponsor — if on employment visa

Additional documents if applicable:

  • Professional certificates — for regulated professional activities
  • Initial approval from sector regulator — for certain activities like healthcare and education
  • Office tenancy contract — for mainland licenses requiring dedicated office space
  • MOA — for companies with multiple shareholders
