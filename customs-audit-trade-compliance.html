Customs audit explained

When do you need a customs audit?

01

To reconcile your transactions

A company that imports and exports often builds up many transactions. We take the details of all of them, match them with your stock and records, and give management one clear picture.

02

When the company is closing down

When a company is closing down, customs asks for a full in and out audit and it must be submitted. If the stock was not recorded properly, customs asks for the documents to clear the open items. We help you prepare them and submit the audit.

Documents required

What documents do you need for a customs audit in UAE?

Standard documents:

  • Trade licence copy
  • Import and export transaction details for the period
  • Stock records, opening and closing
  • Commercial invoices and packing lists
  • Bills of lading or airway bills
  • Customs duty payment records

Additional documents:

  • Free zone gate passes, where goods moved through a free zone
  • Earlier customs letters or queries, if any
