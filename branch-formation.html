Branch types

What types of branch offices does Modest Consulting Services set up in UAE?

01

UAE mainland branch of a foreign company

An international company establishes a branch office registered with the Ministry of Economy and the relevant emirate authority — DET Dubai, ADDED Abu Dhabi, or SEDD Sharjah. The branch operates under the foreign parent company's name and conducts identical or similar activities. A UAE national local agent is required — this is a mandatory legal requirement and does not affect ownership.

02

UAE mainland branch of a UAE company

An existing UAE mainland company opens a branch in another emirate or another location within the same emirate. The branch operates under the parent UAE company's license and trade name. Useful for expanding operations geographically without creating a new independent entity.

03

Free zone branch of a mainland company

A UAE mainland company establishes a branch within a free zone — JAFZA, DMCC, IFZA, or others — to access free zone benefits for specific activities while maintaining the mainland entity separately.

04

Branch of a GCC company

A company registered in another GCC country — Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman, or Qatar — sets up a branch in UAE. Subject to bilateral trade and investment agreements between UAE and the country of origin.

Documents required

What documents do you need for branch formation in UAE?

Standard documents — all branch types:

  • Parent company certificate of incorporation — attested
  • Parent company Memorandum and Articles of Association — attested
  • Board resolution authorising the UAE branch — attested
  • Power of Attorney appointing the UAE branch manager — attested
  • Branch manager passport copy
  • Proposed branch trade name

Additional documents — foreign company branches:

  • UAE Embassy attestation or Apostille certification from the home country
  • Ministry of Foreign Affairs UAE attestation
  • Ministry of Economy UAE registration application

Additional documents — UAE company branches:

  • Parent company valid trade license copy
  • Shareholder resolution authorising the new branch
  • Proposed branch address — Ejari or free zone address
