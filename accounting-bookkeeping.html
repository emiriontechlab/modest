What do you need to get started with accounting in UAE?

Standard documents:

  • Trade license copy
  • MoA — company memorandum of association
  • Bank account statements — last 3–6 months for all business accounts
  • Sales invoices — all customer invoices issued
  • Purchase invoices — all supplier invoices received

Additional documents if applicable:

  • Previous financial statements — for backlog cleanup
  • VAT registration certificate — if VAT-registered
  • Payroll records — if integrating with payroll service
  • Loan or lease agreements — for liability recording
  • Fixed asset list — equipment, vehicles, property owned
