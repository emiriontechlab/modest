DOCUMENTS REQUIRED

What do you need to get started with accounting in the UAE?

Standard documents:

  • Trade licence copy
  • Memorandum of association
  • Bank statements for the last 3 to 6 months, for every business account
  • Sales invoices issued to customers
  • Purchase invoices received from suppliers

Extra documents if they apply:

  • Previous financial statements, for backlog clean-up
  • VAT registration certificate, if VAT-registered
  • Corporate Tax registration, if already registered
  • Payroll records, if we also run your payroll
  • Loan or lease agreements
  • Fixed asset list: equipment, vehicles and property
