Understanding WPS

What is WPS and why does it matter for your business?

01

What is WPS?

WPS — Wages Protection System — is a UAE government-mandated electronic salary transfer system that ensures salaries are paid on time and in full. All UAE private sector companies registered with MOHRE must pay employee salaries through WPS every month.

02

How does WPS work?

Employers prepare a Salary Information File — SIF — in the exact format specified by the UAE Central Bank and submit it to their bank for processing. The bank transfers salaries and confirms the transaction to MOHRE.

03

What are the WPS penalties?

Month 1 non-compliance: MOHRE warning and investigation. Month 2: Ban on new work permit applications. Month 3: Company placed on MOHRE stop-list — no new visas, no government services. Fine: AED 1,000 per employee per month for salary delays exceeding 10 days.

04

Does WPS apply to free zone companies?

WPS is mandatory for all MOHRE-registered companies. Most mainland companies are MOHRE-registered. Free zone companies process visas through their free zone authority — salary protection requirements vary by zone. Modest Consulting Services advises on your specific jurisdiction's requirements.
