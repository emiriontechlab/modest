Liquidation types

What types of company liquidation does Modest Consulting Services handle?

01

Voluntary liquidation — mainland company

The shareholders of a mainland UAE company decide to close the business voluntarily. The process involves a board resolution, appointment of a liquidator, public notice publication in two UAE newspapers, creditor settlement period, authority approvals, and final deregistration with DET, ADDED, or SEDD.

⏱ 2–4 months | Dubai (DET) · Abu Dhabi (ADDED) · Sharjah (SEDD)
02

Free zone company liquidation

Free zone companies are liquidated through their specific free zone authority — DMCC, JAFZA, DAFZA, Dubai South, IFZA, SHAMS, Ajman Free Zone, or RAKEZ. Each free zone has its own liquidation procedure and checklist. Free zone liquidation is generally faster than mainland liquidation.

⏱ 1–3 months | JAFZA · Dubai South · DMCC · DAFZA · IFZA · SHAMS · Ajman Free Zone · RAKEZ
03

Offshore company liquidation

Offshore companies registered in JAFZA or RAKEZ are liquidated through the respective offshore registry. A simpler process than mainland liquidation with fewer public notice requirements.

⏱ 4–8 weeks for offshore liquidation
04

Dormant company closure

A company that has been inactive for an extended period — no operations, no employees, no transactions — follows a simplified closure procedure in some jurisdictions. Modest Consulting Services advises on whether your company qualifies for a simplified process.

Documents required

What documents do you need to liquidate a company in UAE?

Standard documents — all liquidation types:

  • Current trade license copy — valid or expired
  • MoA copy — full memorandum of association
  • Passport copies — all shareholders and managers
  • Emirates ID copies — all UAE resident shareholders and managers
  • List of all employees with visa and Emirates ID numbers

Additional documents — mainland liquidation:

  • No-objection letter from all shareholders confirming liquidation agreement
  • Bank account closure confirmation or no-outstanding-liabilities letter from bank
  • FTA clearance certificate — if VAT-registered
  • MOHRE clearance certificate — if employees were registered

Additional documents — free zone liquidation:

  • Free zone authority liquidation application form
  • Audited financial statements — required by most free zones
  • Lease cancellation confirmation from office or flexi-desk provider
