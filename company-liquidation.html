LIQUIDATION TYPES

What types of company liquidation do we handle?

01

Voluntary liquidation: mainland company

The shareholders decide to close the company. The steps are a shareholder resolution, appointment of a registered liquidator, a public notice in local newspapers (one in Arabic) giving creditors 45 days to come forward, settlement of debts, clearances, and final deregistration with DET in Dubai, ADDED in Abu Dhabi or SEDD in Sharjah.

⏳ Depends on the authority · Dubai (DET) · Abu Dhabi (ADDED) · Sharjah (SEDD)
02

Free zone company liquidation

Each free zone has its own closure checklist, including DMCC, JAFZA, DAFZA, Dubai South, IFZA, SHAMS, Ajman Free Zone and RAKEZ. Most ask for audited accounts, a lease cancellation and a public notice. We follow your free zone's exact list.

⏳ Depends on the free zone · JAFZA · Dubai South · DMCC · DAFZA · IFZA · SHAMS · Ajman Free Zone · RAKEZ
03

Offshore company liquidation

Offshore companies registered with JAFZA Offshore or RAK ICC are closed through their registry, usually with fewer steps than a mainland company.

⏳ Depends on the registry
04

Dormant company closure

A company with no trading, staff or transactions for a long time still has to be closed formally. Some authorities allow a shorter process. We check whether yours qualifies and clear any fines first.

DOCUMENTS REQUIRED

What documents do you need to liquidate a company in the UAE?

Standard documents, all liquidation types:

  • Current trade licence copy, valid or expired
  • Memorandum of association and any amendments
  • Passport copies of all shareholders and the manager
  • Emirates ID copies of UAE resident shareholders and the manager
  • List of employees with visa and Emirates ID details

Extra documents for mainland liquidation:

  • Shareholder resolution approving the liquidation (we draft it)
  • Bank letter confirming account closure or no outstanding liabilities
  • FTA clearance for VAT and Corporate Tax deregistration
  • MOHRE clearance, if employees were registered
  • Liquidator's final report (prepared by the liquidator)

Extra documents for free zone liquidation:

  • Free zone liquidation application form
  • Audited financial statements (most free zones)
  • Lease cancellation from the office or flexi-desk provider
