Most Free Zone company formations are completed in 3–7 working days, while Mainland setups typically take 5–10 working days depending on the business activity and approvals required.

Free Zone companies offer 100% foreign ownership and are ideal for international trade, while Mainland companies can trade directly within the UAE market without restrictions.

Most Mainland business activities now allow 100% foreign ownership under the current UAE Commercial Companies Law, though certain strategic sectors may still require a local partner.

VAT registration through the FTA is free, but businesses typically engage a consultant to ensure accurate registration and ongoing compliant filing, helping avoid penalties caused by errors.
