How long does company formation take in the UAE?

Most free zone companies are formed in 3–7 working days. Mainland companies usually take 5–10 working days, depending on the activity and approvals needed.

Should I choose a free zone or a mainland company?

Choose a free zone for 100% foreign ownership, international trade and lower set-up cost. Choose mainland to trade directly with UAE customers and bid for government contracts. We compare both for your activity in the free consultation.

Do I need a local sponsor in Dubai?

No, for most activities. Most mainland activities allow 100% foreign ownership under the UAE Commercial Companies Law, but some strategic activities still need a UAE national partner.

How much does it cost to close a company in the UAE?

It depends on the authority and on whether visas and bank accounts must be closed. As a guide, free zone liquidation typically costs AED 3,000–7,000 and mainland liquidation AED 5,000–12,000 in professional and government fees. We give a fixed written quote after reviewing your licence.

Can you set up my company if I am outside the UAE?

Yes. Most free zone setups can be completed remotely; we tell you if a visit is needed for visa medicals or bank account opening.
