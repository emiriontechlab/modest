Types of amendments

What changes can you make to a UAE trade license?

You can change your trade name, add or remove business activities, add or remove shareholders, change the manager or authorised signatory, move your registered address, convert your legal structure, or increase or decrease share capital. Each change is filed as an amendment with your licensing authority.

01

Trade name change

New name search, authority approval and updated licence. Keep using your old name until the amended licence is issued.

02

Business activity addition or removal

Add new activities or remove ones you no longer use. Some regulated activities need a sector regulator's approval first. Need a brand-new licence instead? See trade license services in Dubai.

03

Shareholder and partner changes

Add or remove a shareholder, or change ownership percentages. Selling shares? See our share transfer services.

04

Manager and authorised signatory change

Update the registered manager or the signatories on your licence. New manager needs a visa? Our PRO and government services team handles it.

05

Company address change

Moving office? Mainland companies register a new tenancy contract (Ejari) and file an address amendment with DET. Free zone companies file it with their free zone authority.

06

Legal structure conversion

For example, from a sole establishment to an LLC. Setting up a new entity instead? See mainland and free zone company setup.

07

Share capital change

Increase or decrease registered share capital, with the resolutions and documents your authority asks for.

Documents required

What documents do I need to amend a trade license in Dubai?

To amend a trade license in Dubai you usually need a copy of your current trade licence, passport copies of all shareholders and managers, Emirates ID copies for UAE residents and your current MOA. Extra documents depend on the change, such as a new tenancy contract for an address change.

Standard documents:

  • Existing trade license copy: valid current license
  • Passport copies: all shareholders and managers
  • Emirates ID copies: UAE residents
  • Current MOA: for amendments affecting company structure

Additional documents by amendment type:

  • Trade name change: new name options (3 recommended), NOC from shareholders
  • Activity addition: description of new activity, professional certificates if regulated
  • Shareholder change: sale and purchase agreement or gift agreement, board resolution, updated share distribution schedule
  • Corporate shareholder: certificate of incorporation, MOA or articles, board resolution and good standing certificate, attested in the home country and in the UAE
  • Address change: new tenancy contract or Ejari registration, NOC from new landlord
  • Manager change: new manager passport and Emirates ID, board resolution appointing new manager

Free zone requirements differ. For example, JAFZA handles activity amendments online through the Dubai Trade portal (JAFZA guide). We confirm your exact list in a free call.
