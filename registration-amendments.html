Documents required

What documents do you need for a company amendment in UAE?

Standard documents:

  • Existing trade license copy — valid current license
  • Passport copies — all shareholders and managers
  • Emirates ID copies — UAE residents
  • Current MOA — for amendments affecting company structure

Additional documents by amendment type:

  • Trade name change: New name options (3 recommended), NOC from shareholders
  • Activity addition: Description of new activity, professional certificates if regulated
  • Shareholder change: Sale and purchase agreement or gift agreement, board resolution, updated share distribution schedule
  • Address change: New tenancy contract or Ejari registration, NOC from new landlord
  • Manager change: New manager passport and Emirates ID, board resolution appointing new manager
