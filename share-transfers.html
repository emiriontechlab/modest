Transfer types

What types of share transfer can you make in the UAE?

In the UAE you can sell part or all of your shares, add a new individual or corporate shareholder, remove a shareholder, or transfer shares to heirs. Each type needs an amended MOA or articles and approval from your licensing authority.

01

Partial share transfer

A shareholder sells part of their shares to a new investor or an existing partner. The company keeps trading; only the ownership split changes.

02

Full share transfer

A shareholder sells all their shares, to an outside buyer or an existing partner. In practice this is the sale of their stake in the business.

03

Adding a new shareholder

A new investor joins by buying existing shares or taking up new ones. The MOA, shareholder register and licence are updated.

04

Removing a shareholder

A shareholder exits by selling their shares or through a buy-out by the other partners.

05

Corporate shareholder

Another company becomes a shareholder. Its incorporation documents and board resolution are needed, usually attested in its home country and in the UAE.

06

Inheritance transfer

Shares pass to heirs after a shareholder dies. This needs court or probate documents; we work alongside your lawyer on the company filings.

Documents required

What documents do you need for a share transfer in Dubai?

For a share transfer in Dubai you usually need your current trade licence and MOA, passport copies of all current and incoming shareholders, Emirates ID copies for UAE residents, and a resolution approving the transfer. Corporate shareholders and inheritance transfers need the extra documents below.

Standard documents for all share transfers:

  • Current trade licence copy
  • Current MOA copy (full memorandum of association)
  • Passport copies of all current and incoming shareholders
  • Emirates ID copies for UAE resident shareholders
  • Board or partner resolution approving the transfer

Additional documents for a new individual shareholder:

  • New shareholder passport copy
  • New shareholder Emirates ID (if UAE resident)
  • NOC from current sponsor (if new shareholder is on UAE employment visa)

Additional documents for a new corporate shareholder:

  • Corporate shareholder certificate of incorporation (attested)
  • Corporate shareholder MOA (attested)
  • Board resolution of the corporate shareholder authorising the investment

Additional documents for an inheritance transfer:

  • Court order or probate documentation
  • Death certificate of the deceased shareholder (attested)
  • Heir documentation as required by UAE succession court

Free zones set their own lists. DMCC, for example, publishes its own share transfer guidelines. We confirm your exact list in a free call.
