Transfer types

What types of share transfers does Modest Consulting Services handle?

01

Partial share transfer — selling a percentage of ownership

An existing shareholder sells a portion of their shares to a new investor or existing co-shareholder. The company continues to operate — only the ownership split changes. Requires updated MoA and authority approval.

02

Full share transfer — complete ownership change

An existing shareholder sells 100% of their shares — either to an external buyer or to an existing co-shareholder. Effectively a business sale in terms of ownership. Requires full MoA restatement and authority approval.

03

New shareholder addition

A new investor joins the company by acquiring newly issued shares or purchasing existing shares from a current shareholder. Requires updated MoA, new shareholder documentation, and authority submission.

04

Shareholder removal

An existing shareholder exits the company — either by selling their shares or through a buy-out. Requires board resolution, updated MoA, and authority approval.

05

Corporate shareholder addition

A corporate entity — another company rather than an individual — becomes a shareholder. Requires additional corporate documentation including the corporate shareholder's certificate of incorporation and MoA, plus board resolution.

06

Inheritance and succession transfer

Shares transfer to heirs following the death of a shareholder. A specific and legally sensitive process governed by UAE succession law. Requires court documentation, probate documents, and authority submission.

Documents required

What documents do you need for a share transfer in UAE?

Standard documents — all share transfers:

  • Current trade license copy
  • Current MoA copy — full memorandum of association
  • Passport copies — all current and incoming shareholders
  • Emirates ID copies — UAE resident shareholders
  • Board resolution approving the share transfer

Additional documents — new individual shareholder:

  • New shareholder passport copy
  • New shareholder Emirates ID — if UAE resident
  • NOC from current sponsor — if new shareholder is on UAE employment visa

Additional documents — new corporate shareholder:

  • Corporate shareholder certificate of incorporation — attested
  • Corporate shareholder MoA — attested
  • Board resolution of the corporate shareholder authorising the investment

Additional documents — inheritance transfer:

  • Court order or probate documentation
  • Death certificate of deceased shareholder — attested
  • Heir documentation — as required by UAE succession court
