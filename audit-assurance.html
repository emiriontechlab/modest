Types of audit

What types of audit does Modest Consulting Services provide?

01

Statutory audit

Legally required annual review of your company's financial statements. Required by most UAE free zones — DMCC, JAFZA, DAFZA, Dubai South, IFZA, SHAMS, Ajman Free Zone, RAKEZ — and prepared to the specific format and standard of each authority.

02

Internal audit

Independent review of your internal financial controls, risk management processes, and operational procedures. Recommended for any business that wants assurance that internal processes are robust and financial risks are controlled.

03

Compliance audit

Verifies that your business meets the specific regulatory requirements of your jurisdiction — MOF standards, FTA VAT regulations, free zone authority rules, or industry-specific requirements. Valuable before regulatory inspections or authority renewals.

04

Due diligence audit

Detailed financial review for a specific transaction — pre-acquisition review for a buyer, investor due diligence, or partnership assessment. Goes beyond statutory audit to examine specific financial risks and opportunities.

05

Special purpose audit

Prepared for a specific defined purpose — bank loan submission, tender requirement, legal proceeding, or regulatory application. Prepared to the exact format and scope requested by the recipient.

Documents required

What documents do you need for an audit in UAE?

Standard documents:

  • Trade license copy
  • MoA — memorandum of association
  • Bank statements — full financial year, all accounts
  • Trial balance — as at financial year end
  • General ledger — all transactions for the year
  • Sales and purchase invoices — complete listing
  • Fixed asset register

Additional documents if applicable:

  • Previous year audit report — for comparative figures
  • Loan agreements and lease contracts
  • VAT returns — full financial year if VAT-registered
  • Payroll records — for staff cost verification
  • Shareholder agreements — for due diligence audits
