Trakhees Approval Process in Dubai: Steps, Documents and Common Mistakes

By Modest Consulting Team · Published November 2026 · Updated November 2026

Yes, you need Trakhees approval if your business is located inside a PCFC-designated zone in Dubai. Trakhees is the licensing and regulatory arm of the Ports, Customs and Free Zone Corporation (PCFC). The approval runs in three main stages: trade name reservation, initial approval, and final licence issuance. Missing documents cause most delays.

Do you need Trakhees approval for your business?

Yes, if your office or warehouse is in a PCFC or Nakheel-managed zone. No, if your business is located in standard Dubai Mainland (DET) or an independent free zone like DMCC or IFZA. Always check your tenancy contract or confirm with your landlord before applying, as your physical address decides your licensing authority.

Trakhees manages three core areas under PCFC:

  • Commercial Licensing (trade licences, renewals, and amendments)
  • Civil Engineering (building permits and fit-out approvals)
  • Environment, Health and Safety (EHS) (industrial and safety clearances)

This guide covers the Commercial Licensing process. (Source: PCFC, About Trakhees - https://pcfc.ae/en/Pages/aboutus-trakhees.aspx)

Which Trakhees approvals does a company need?

For a standard business setup, Trakhees requires three core approvals: trade name approval, initial approval, and final licence registration.

Depending on your business activity, you may also need external third-party approvals:

  • Dubai Maritime Authority NOC: Required for marine and shipping activities.
  • DTCM Approval: Required for hotels, holiday homes, and tourism services.
  • Sponsor NOC: Required if a partner or manager holds a UAE residence or visit visa under another sponsor.
Note: Fit-out, building, and EHS permits are separate technical approvals handled through an approved engineering consultant. (Source: PCFC New Free Zone Licence Procedure - https://www.pcfc.ae/en/Service%20Documents/PCFC-TRK-LD-LS-CP-01,%20Issuance%20New%20Free%20Zone%20License.pdf)

Trakhees Licensing vs. Trakhees Technical Permits: What is the difference?

Item Trakhees Commercial Licensing Trakhees Engineering & EHS Permits
Purpose To legally register your company and issue a trade licence. To approve office fit-outs, construction, or warehouse safety.
When is it needed? Before starting business operations. Before renovating an office or operating industrial equipment.
Who handles it? Business setup consultants / PRO teams. Approved engineering or fit-out consultants.
Output document Trade Licence, Certificate of Formation, Share Certificate. Building and EHS approvals.

What documents do you need for Trakhees approval?

To start your Trakhees licence application, you need passport copies, Emirates IDs, signed application forms, a board resolution, the Memorandum of Association (MoA), and an Ejari or lease agreement. Extra corporate papers apply if another company owns shares in your business.

Documents Required for Individual Owners & Managers

  • Passport copy of every owner, partner, and manager
  • Emirates ID and UAE visa copy (for UAE residents)
  • Entry stamp or visit visa copy (for non-residents)
  • Current sponsor NOC (if applicable)
  • Signed Trade Name Reservation and Registration forms
  • Beneficial Owner Declaration (UBO form)
  • Signed Acknowledgement and Undertaking form
  • Lease contract, developer letter, or Ejari certificate

Additional Documents for Corporate Shareholders or Branches

  • Board Resolution approving the new setup (attested)
  • Parent company Trade Licence and Certificate of Incorporation
  • Parent company Memorandum and Articles of Association (MoA)
  • Attested and legally translated Arabic copies (if the parent company is outside the UAE)
  • Parent company NOC (for branch setups only)
Note: Drafting legal documents such as the Memorandum and Articles of Association is coordinated by Modest Consulting through registered UAE legal partners.

What is the step-by-step Trakhees approval process?

The Trakhees licensing process follows six sequential steps. Each step requires official approval before the next stage unlocks in the portal.

  1. Verify your zone: Confirm with your landlord or developer that your premises fall under Trakhees jurisdiction.
  2. Reserve your trade name: Submit your trade name through the Trakhees online portal or Service Happiness Centre.
  3. Sign legal documents: All partners and the manager must attend with original passports to sign the Board Resolution and MoA.
  4. Obtain Initial Approval: Trakhees reviews your file and conducts security checks before emailing your Initial Approval Certificate.
  5. Submit external approvals: If your activity requires a NOC (such as DTCM or Maritime Authority), upload it after paying the initial approval fee.
  6. Submit Ejari and receive your licence: Upload your registered lease, pay the registration and licence vouchers, and receive your digital Trade Licence, Share Certificate, and Certificate of Formation.

What affects the cost of Trakhees approval?

Trakhees follows an official government fee tariff, so exact totals vary per company. Your final setup cost depends on five main factors:

  • Your specific business activity and external NOC fees
  • Your legal structure (FZE for single owner vs. FZCO for multiple partners)
  • Your facility type (Flexi-desk, commercial office, or warehouse)
  • Your visa quota requirements (employee and investor visas)
  • Legal translation and attestation fees for foreign corporate documents

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does Trakhees approval take?

There is no fixed number of days for Trakhees approval. Processing speed depends entirely on how complete your paperwork is and whether your activity requires third-party NOCs. Files with complete, properly attested documents move smoothly through each stage, while missing papers pause the application until corrected.

Why do Trakhees applications get rejected?

Incomplete documentation and restricted trade names are the most common causes of Trakhees rejections. If an application is rejected at any stage, you must fix the error and resubmit before moving forward.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Choosing a restricted trade name: Names containing country names, government terms, offensive words, punctuation marks, or words unrelated to your activity are automatically rejected.
  • Submitting unattested foreign documents: Corporate documents issued outside the UAE must be notarised, UAE-embassy attested, and legally translated into Arabic.
  • Missing original passports at signing: Every signatory must appear in person with their original passport during the MoA signing stage.
  • Appointing an underage manager or owner: All shareholders and managers must be at least 18 years old.
  • Insufficient portal balance: Online submissions fail if your Trakhees pre-paid account lacks sufficient funds for the transaction fee.
