PCFC — Ports, Customs and Free Zone Corporation — is a major Dubai government entity that oversees several economic zones and ports including JAFZA (Jebel Ali Free Zone) and Trakhees zones. Businesses operating within PCFC-regulated areas are licensed and regulated by the relevant PCFC entity.

Trakhees is the Department of Planning and Development under PCFC. It provides licensing, regulatory oversight, and civil engineering approvals for businesses operating in PCFC-designated development zones and communities across Dubai — including Dragon Mart, Discovery Gardens, and other PCFC-managed areas.

Businesses operating within Trakhees-regulated development zones require a Trakhees commercial, industrial, or professional license rather than a standard DET mainland or free zone license. Modest Consulting Services advises on whether your business location and activity require a Trakhees license.

Yes. JAFZA is one of the key free zones we cover. We manage the complete JAFZA company formation, licensing, and ongoing compliance — including renewal and amendment services.

A standard Trakhees commercial license takes 5–10 working days from application submission for activities that do not require physical inspection. Activities requiring EHS approval or civil engineering inspection may take 2–4 weeks.
