What type of company do you want to set up in UAE?

UAE offers three main company types. Choosing the wrong one limits your business. Choosing the right one sets you up for long-term success. Modest Consulting Services advises you on the best option before we submit anything.

Most Popular

Mainland Company Formation

A mainland company registers directly with the UAE government authority — DET in Dubai, ADDED in Abu Dhabi, or SEDD in Sharjah. You can trade freely anywhere in UAE and internationally with no restrictions.

Since the 2021 law change, foreigners own 100% of most mainland companies — no UAE national partner required for the majority of activities. Mainland is the right choice if you want to work with UAE government departments, bid on government tenders, or trade freely across the UAE market.

Free Zone Company Formation

Free zone companies register with a specific free zone authority — not the UAE government. They offer 100% foreign ownership, fast registration, and lower setup costs. The key limitation is that free zone companies cannot trade directly within UAE mainland without appointing a mainland distributor.

If your customers are primarily outside UAE or in other free zones, a free zone entity is the most cost-effective and fastest option.

Documents required

What documents do you need for company formation in UAE?

Standard documents:

  • Passport copy — all shareholders and managers, minimum 6 months validity
  • UAE visa copy or entry stamp — if currently in UAE
  • Emirates ID copy — for UAE residents
  • NOC from current sponsor — if on UAE employment visa
  • Proposed trade name — 3 options in order of preference
  • Business activity description — what your company does

Additional documents — mainland:

  • Professional qualification certificates — for doctors, engineers, architects, consultants
  • Local sponsor details — only for activities that still require a UAE national service agent
  • Office tenancy contract Ejari — for activities requiring a physical office

Additional documents — free zone:

  • Free zone application form — specific to each zone, we prepare this for you
  • Business plan — required by some free zones for certain activities

Additional documents — offshore:

  • Source of funds declaration
  • Bank reference letter
  • Board resolution and POA — if corporate shareholders are involved
