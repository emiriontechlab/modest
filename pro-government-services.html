DOCUMENTS REQUIRED

What documents do you need for PRO services in the UAE?

Standard documents, visa processing:

  • Passport copy, valid for at least 6 months
  • Current visa or entry stamp, and Emirates ID copy if you live in the UAE
  • Recent passport-size photo, white background
  • Company trade licence and establishment card copies

Extra documents, employment visa:

  • Attested educational certificate (for skilled job titles)
  • Signed offer letter or employment contract
  • Health insurance certificate
  • Cancellation of the previous visa, if transferring from another employer

Extra documents, investor or partner visa:

  • Memorandum of association
  • Trade licence or share certificate showing your ownership

Extra documents, family visa:

  • Attested marriage certificate and children's birth certificates
  • Registered tenancy contract (Ejari in Dubai)
  • Salary certificate or employment contract

Extra documents, document attestation:

  • Original document
  • Passport copy of the document holder
